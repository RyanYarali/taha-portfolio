Case study 03 · Business analysis

Data Governance Case Analysis

A business that couldn't agree on its own numbers. I worked out why, showed how each data problem affected decisions, and mapped who should own the fix.

Role
Business analysis
Methods
Governance case analysis, RACI
Type
BCIT coursework
Focus
Ownership, quality, privacy

The problem

Revenue didn't match across reports, customers couldn't be reliably identified, and different teams were working from different versions of the same file.

These weren't just technical bugs. Nobody clearly owned the data, so nobody owned the problems either.

Key takeaways

Root causeOwnership before toolingMost issues traced back to unclear ownership. A new tool wouldn't fix data nobody is accountable for.
ImpactQuality is a business issueEvery data problem led to a decision problem: trusting revenue, knowing customers, staying compliant.
MethodOne accountable person per taskA RACI matrix makes it explicit who does the work, who signs off and who just needs to know.
PrivacyGovernance protects people tooClear rules for customer data reduce compliance risk and protect trust, not just accuracy.
