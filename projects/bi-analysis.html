Case study 02 · Business intelligence

BI Analysis: Spotify, Airbnb & Climate

Three different datasets with the same job each time: turn raw rows into answers. I used filtering, aggregation and comparison in Excel and Power BI, and led the group that delivered it.

Role
Analyst & group lead
Tools
Power BI, Excel
Data
Spotify Top 10,000 · Airbnb · Abbotsford climate
Type
BCIT coursework

The problem

Each dataset came with analytical questions that couldn't be answered by looking at the raw table. The Spotify file alone has 10,000 rows.

The work was to shape the data so the answer is obvious, then communicate it so someone non-technical could act on it.

What I did

  1. Filtered to what mattersNarrowed each dataset to the rows and fields that actually answer the question.
  2. AggregatedGrouped and summarized records into totals, averages and counts.
  3. ComparedPut groups side by side to surface the differences that answer the question.
  4. Visualized & explainedBuilt the views in Excel and Power BI and presented the findings.
  5. Led the groupCoordinated tasks, tracked progress and ran reviews so the team delivered on time.

The method

Illustration of the method. Questions are examples, and bar heights are not the real results.

Key takeaways

MethodSame playbook, three domainsFilter, aggregate, compare worked for music, housing and weather data alike. The questions changed, not the method.
ToolingExcel to explore, Power BI to explainExcel was quickest for exploring, and Power BI was better for presenting findings clearly.
LeadershipKept a group on trackI coordinated tasks, tracked progress and ran reviews, making sure the analysis was checked before it was shared.
CommunicationAnswers, not just chartsEvery visual was tied back to the question it answers.

  • Comparative analysis
